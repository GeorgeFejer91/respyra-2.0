Phone remote control

Enable a private link, scan the QR code, then select Connect on your phone. The connected phone can edit setup, select LSL input, start or stop the experiment, and view its status.

This shares participant and session fields with the connected phone. Keep the link private. Internet access is required; the invitation expires after four hours.

Phone control disabled.

You can also load this link in the Recorder’s Add external page tab. Disable phone control to revoke the link. Disconnecting the phone leaves an ongoing experiment running.
